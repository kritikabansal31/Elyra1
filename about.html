ELYRA is an Indian fashion label built on one idea — that clothing should feel as natural as skin, not as loud as noise.

How ELYRA began

ELYRA was founded in 2018 by Aanya Mehra, a Mumbai-based designer who trained for six years under master tailors in Milan and Paris. She returned to India with one obsession — to create clothes that drape, not just fit.

What started as a small atelier in Kala Ghoda with two tailors and a single sewing table has grown into a full fashion house covering womenswear, menswear, kidswear, jewellery, footwear, accessories, and beauty. Every product ELYRA makes still begins the same way: on a dress form, by hand.

We are not a fast-fashion brand. We do not chase trends. We make things that belong in your wardrobe for ten years — not ten days.

What ELYRA stands for

◇

Slow Fashion

We release two collections a year — not fifty-two. Each piece is designed, cut, stitched, and finished by artisans who sign their work. We believe the person who made your clothes should be proud of what they made.

◈

Indian Craft

ELYRA works with weavers in Varanasi, block printers in Jaipur, and leather workers in Kolkata. Every category carries a thread of traditional Indian craft, updated for the way people dress today.

◉

Responsible Making

We source deadstock fabrics, use natural dyes where possible, and pack all orders in recycled materials. Our goal is to reach zero waste in production by 2027 — we are currently at 34%.

◎

Size Inclusion

Every ELYRA garment is available from XS to 4XL at the same price. We do not charge more for more fabric. Bodies are different. Clothing should be too.
